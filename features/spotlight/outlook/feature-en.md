# Outlook on iPhone Duo: Halfway There

**DuoReady Spotlight · No. 1 — Microsoft Outlook**
Score: **3/5 (60/100)** · Status: Partially adapted · Sources verified 2026-10-29

## The one-line summary

Everyone says Outlook is "fully adapted" for iPhone Duo. We checked: Microsoft kept the launch-day half of the promise — Outlook already fills the Duo's big screen properly — but the feature people actually want, the inbox sitting next to a full message, is still an Apple-promised "coming weeks" feature that has not shipped. A week after launch, nobody has filmed Outlook on a Duo, because the headline layout isn't out yet.

Think of it this way: on a slab phone, reading email is like reading a newspaper folded to a single column — you keep unfolding and refolding. The Duo is supposed to lay the paper flat on the table: index on the left, article on the right. Today, Outlook gives you a bigger sheet of paper. The flat-open layout is still coming.

## What you get today (verified)

- **Official Duo support shipped on launch day.** Microsoft's own release notes for Outlook iOS 5.2641.0 (October 23, 2026) state: "Added support for iPhone Duo, with layouts optimized for side-by-side multitasking and smoother resizing during orientation and window-size changes."
- **It fills the inner display** — no letterboxing, built on Apple's Duo APIs (iOS 27.1 SDK), confirmed on builds 5.2641.0 and 5.2642.0 by independent build analysis (FitsDuo).
- **Real split-screen works**: Outlook can sit side by side with another app (calendar, Teams, notes) — on a slab iPhone this simply isn't possible.

## What's still missing (verified)

- **The two-pane inbox.** Apple's Newsroom (October 22) describes what's coming: "In the coming weeks, Microsoft Word and Outlook will roll out a desktop-class experience… Outlook's inbox next to a full message." As of October 29, no source — official or otherwise — confirms this layout has shipped. The October 26 update (5.2642.0) mentions only "performance improvements and bug fixes."
- **You can't discover Duo support from the App Store.** Neither update's App Store notes mention the Duo at all; the only first-party record is Microsoft's developer release-notes page.
- **Calendar, compose, and half-fold behavior are undocumented.** No owner reports, no hands-on video, no official screenshot of Outlook on a Duo exists anywhere we could find, in English or Chinese. We score what is proven, not what is previewed.

## Slab vs. Duo, honestly

| | Slab iPhone | iPhone Duo today | iPhone Duo, coming weeks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reading mail | One column: list → tap → full message → back | Same flow, on a much bigger canvas; second app alongside | Inbox + full message side by side (Apple's preview) |
| Multitasking | One app at a time | Two apps side by side | Same, plus the in-app two-pane |

## Our verdict

**3/5 — a real, official, partial adaptation.** The foundation is genuinely there (launch-day support is more than most apps managed), but the defining Duo experience for a mail app is the two-pane inbox, and that is still a promise with a date range, not a date. When a build newer than 5.2642.0 ships the two-pane layout and owners confirm it, we will re-test and re-score this page — that's the deal with every DuoReady featured review.

**What to watch:** the next Outlook iOS release notes. If the words "two-pane" or "inbox" appear next to "iPhone Duo," the second half of this story lands.
