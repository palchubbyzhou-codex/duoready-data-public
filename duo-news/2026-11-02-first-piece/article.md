## ENGLISH

# The Duo Was Never Supposed to Launch in October

**Apple's own plan was November, two countries, and shelves that could actually hold stock. Duo owners got the opposite — and if you've been refreshing store pages at dawn wondering why, this week brought the answer, plus the two iOS updates where the fixes live.**

![hero](hero.png)

### The launch that slipped its own schedule

Apple originally intended to hold the iPhone Duo until November, and to sell it only in the U.S. and China while supply caught up, according to Bloomberg's Mark Gurman. Instead it launched on October 23 in more than 70 countries at once. Gurman's verdict is blunt: Apple rushed the Duo to market without enough inventory to meet demand. Online delivery estimates now sit at January 25.

The detail that matters most for anyone still hunting: roughly 80% of the initial inventory went to Apple's retail stores, not the online store — and some locations held as few as 50 units on day one. The store shelf, not the shipping queue, is where this launch actually happened.

Our own tracker can see the shape of that decision. DuoReady has watched all 254 U.S. Apple Stores since October 24 and logged 406 restocks. In the last seven days alone, 244 drops landed across 141 stores, and nearly half of them — 119 — fell inside a single three-hour window, 9 a.m. to noon Eastern. The drops are real, but they are thin and they are fast: on Monday, the morning window produced no U.S. drop at all on our tracker, an afternoon drop in Boca Raton, Florida was gone in five minutes, and the fastest verified sellout of the day took one minute.

That is what a store-first launch with 50-unit shelves looks like from the outside. The scarcity isn't a mystery anymore. It was scheduled.

### One setting you can't change your mind about

Here is a smaller story with a longer tail for owners. On every other iPhone, Settings → Appearance → Display Zoom lets you change how large text and icons appear — Default, Medium, or Large — at any time. On the iPhone Duo, that option simply isn't there after initial setup. The choice you make on the Appearance screen when you first turn the phone on is the choice you keep.

MacRumors confirmed the option is still missing on the current iOS 27.2 software. One owner who asked Apple Support about it was told by a senior advisor that there is no way to change the setting afterwards — the only workaround is to erase the phone and set it up again. Why Apple removed it is unknown; it may be caution around the folding display's layouts, or a plain oversight. Either way, until an update restores it: pick your Appearance setting like it matters. For now, it does.

![zoom](zoom.png)

### iOS 27.2.1: the fix update, now in testing

Apple has begun testing iOS 27.2.1, spotted this week in the visitor logs of MacRumors and 9to5Mac — a signal that has reliably preceded releases by days to a few weeks. Nothing about it suggests new features. Expect a maintenance release: bug fixes and security patches, with fixes for the young iPhone Duo lineup expected to be part of the package.

Whether the Display Zoom toggle returns in 27.2.1 or has to wait for 27.3 is an open question. Our advice is simple: when this one lands, install it the same day. On a first-generation product in its second week, the boring updates are the important ones.

### iOS 27.3: beta this week, and Apple Intelligence reaches China

The bigger update is already dated. The first iOS 27.3 developer beta is expected Tuesday, November 3 in the U.S. — Apple's China newsroom lists the beta starting November 4 in China — with the public release planned for December.

The headline is geographic: iOS 27.3 turns on Apple Intelligence in China for the first time, on iPhone 15 Pro and newer — a list that includes the Duo. Chinese users get Writing Tools, Genmoji, Clean Up, notification summaries, Priority Notifications, Visual Intelligence, and Live Translation through AirPods. Apple is also building Alibaba's Qwen assistant into Siri, Writing Tools, and Visual Intelligence. Two limits worth knowing: the Siri AI features shown at WWDC are not part of the China rollout yet, and the new features are iPhone-only in China at first, pending regulatory approvals for other devices.

For Duo owners in China, in other words, December is the phone's second launch day — the AI half of the device arrives then.

![ai-china](ai-china.png)

### If your Duo cracks: what actually happens

Apple has now notified its stores about cracked iPhone Duos and given them troubleshooting and replacement guidance based on how the damage occurred. The short version, from Apple's own statements: if the screen fails as a defect, the phone is repaired or replaced under the standard process — you do not need AppleCare+ for a defect. Out of warranty, an inner-display replacement is listed at $999 in the U.S.; with AppleCare+, it's $99. One deadline for launch-day buyers: the standard 14-day return window on an October 23 purchase closes November 6.

### Quick hits

- **Old iPhones, important patch:** iOS 18.7.11 for iPhone XS, XS Max, and XR fixes a CoreGraphics flaw Apple says may have been exploited in an extremely sophisticated attack against specific individuals. If a phone in your household is on iOS 18, update it.
- **Apple Watch showing the wrong time after the daylight-saving change?** Apple has published a support document; the fix is to switch to a different watch face, then restart the Watch.
- **iPad mini, Friday:** the new OLED iPad mini with A20 Pro goes on sale November 6. Its EU energy-label filing lists a 4,900 mAh rated battery — the customary ~10 hours of video playback.
- **The cheapest Mac got pricier:** MacBook Neo education pricing rose to $649 this week — from $499 at its March launch — as memory-chip costs climb. The student discount is now $50.
- **A quiet acquisition:** Apple has bought Ascend.io, an agentic data-engineering company whose team is winding down its service. No user-facing changes have been announced.
- **Bigger HomeView, later:** a 9-inch HomeView with a swiveling arm is in the works, but not before 2027 — 2028 is possible.
- **Early 2027, per Gurman:** refreshed iPad Air, iPad Pro (M6), and entry-level iPad, plus a second-generation MacBook Neo in the first half of the year.
- **Today:** Apple reports earnings at 5 p.m. Eastern, the first call with John Ternus as CEO.

# Duo 本不该在十月上市

**苹果原本的计划是：等到 11 月、只在中美两国先卖、货备足了再上。Duo 的用户等到的却是反面——如果你最近天天一早刷门店页面问为什么没货，这周答案出来了；顺带还有两个 iOS 更新，修复都在里面。**

![hero](hero.png)

### 一场提前了的发布

据彭博社 Mark Gurman 报道，苹果最初打算把 iPhone Duo 压到 11 月再发布，而且只在美国和中国先行发售，等产能爬上来再说。结果是 10 月 23 日在 70 多个国家同时开卖。Gurman 的评价很不客气：苹果在库存根本不够的情况下，硬把 Duo 赶上了市。现在官网的发货预期已经排到 1 月 25 日。

对还在找机器的人来说，最关键的一个数字是：首批货源大约 80% 给了线下零售店，而不是官网——有些门店首日只有 50 台。也就是说，这场发布的真正主场在门店货架上，不在快递队列里。

我们自己的追踪器能看见这个决定长什么样。DuoReady 从 10 月 24 日起盯着全美 254 家 Apple Store，一共记录了 406 次补货。光是最近 7 天，就有 244 次掉货、落在 141 家店，其中将近一半——119 次——挤在美东时间上午 9 点到 12 点这三个小时里。货是真的有，但又薄又快：本周一，整个上午的黄金窗口在我们的追踪器上一台没掉；下午佛州 Boca Raton 掉的一批，5 分钟就没了；当天经核实最快的售罄只用了 1 分钟。

这就是「门店优先、每家 50 台」的发布从外面看起来的样子。缺货不再是谜了——它是被排进日程表的。

### 有一个设置，选错了改不回来

再说一件小事，但对机主影响长久。其他任何 iPhone 上，「设置 → 外观 → 显示缩放」都可以随时改文字和图标的大小：默认、中等、大。iPhone Duo 上，这个选项在初始设置之后就不存在了。你第一次开机在外观界面选了什么，就一直是什么。

MacRumors 已确认，在现行 iOS 27.2 系统上该选项依然缺失。一位机主向苹果支持求证后被告知：目前没有办法在设置后更改，唯一的办法是抹掉手机重新设置。苹果为什么拿掉它还不清楚——可能怕折叠屏布局出问题，也可能就是疏漏。无论如何，在更新把它带回来之前：开机那一下，把外观选项当回事选。现在，它真的改不了。

![zoom](zoom.png)

### iOS 27.2.1：修复更新，已经在内测

苹果已经开始测试 iOS 27.2.1——本周它出现在 MacRumors 和 9to5Mac 的访问日志里，这类信号通常意味着正式版在几天到几周内就会到。不要期待新功能：这会是一个维护版更新，打补丁、修 bug，刚上市的 iPhone Duo 的修复预计也在其中。

显示缩放能不能在 27.2.1 里回来，还是要等 27.3，目前没有答案。我们的建议很简单：它一推送，当天就装。第一代产品上市第二周，无聊的更新才是最重要的更新。

### iOS 27.3：本周 beta，Apple Intelligence 正式入华

大更新已经有日期了。iOS 27.3 第一个开发者 beta 预计美东时间 11 月 3 日（周二）放出——苹果中国新闻中心公布的日期是中国时间 11 月 4 日开始测试——正式版计划在 12 月发布。

头条是地域性的：iOS 27.3 将首次在中国开启 Apple Intelligence，支持 iPhone 15 Pro 及更新机型——Duo 在列。中国用户将得到写作工具、Genmoji、消除、通知摘要、重要通知、视觉智能，以及通过 AirPods 的实时翻译。苹果还把阿里的通义千问（Qwen）接进了 Siri、写作工具和视觉智能。两个限制要先知道：WWDC 上演示的 Siri AI 功能这次不在中国上线之列；新功能在中国先只上 iPhone，其他设备还要等审批。

换句话说，对中国的 Duo 机主来说，12 月才是这台手机的第二次发布——它的 AI 那一半，到那时才到货。

### 如果 Duo 的屏幕裂了：实际会怎么处理

苹果已经向门店下发了关于 iPhone Duo 屏幕开裂的通知，给了排查和更换的指导原则。按苹果自己的说法，结论很简单：如果是缺陷导致的屏幕问题，走标准流程维修或换机——缺陷换机不需要买 AppleCare+。保外换内屏，美国官网价 999 美元；有 AppleCare+ 时是 99 美元。给首发买家提一个醒：10 月 23 日买的机器，14 天退货窗口在 11 月 6 日关闭。

### 速览

- **老 iPhone 的重要补丁：** 面向 iPhone XS、XS Max、XR 的 iOS 18.7.11 修复了一个 CoreGraphics 漏洞——苹果称它可能已被用于针对特定个人的极其复杂的攻击。家里有停在 iOS 18 的手机，升上去。

- **最便宜的 Mac 涨价了：** MacBook Neo 教育价本周涨到 649 美元——3 月发布时是 499 美元——内存芯片成本在涨。学生优惠现在只剩 50 美元。

- **2027 年初（据 Gurman）：** 新款 iPad Air、iPad Pro（M6）和入门 iPad，以及上半年发布的第二代 MacBook Neo。
- **今天：** 苹果美东时间下午 5 点发财报，这是 John Ternus 任 CEO 后的第一场电话会。
